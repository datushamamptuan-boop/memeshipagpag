Signal surfaces heuristic risk scores — safety checks, liquidity, socials, and smart-wallet activity — and applies stop-loss / take-profit rules. It is not financial advice, cannot predict price, and cannot guarantee a rug wasn't missed. In Auto mode, trades execute without a per-trade confirmation but are still bounded by the daily loss cap and losing-streak cooldown configured in your repo. You are trading at your own risk.

GitHub connection

Confirming or skipping a trade writes a decision file to your repo and pings the bot to run immediately. Needs a token with write access — kept only in this browser, never sent anywhere but api.github.com.

Don't want to confirm from here at all? Set AUTO_TRADE_MODE=auto as a repo variable/secret instead — the bot will trade the moment a signal clears, no button needed.
